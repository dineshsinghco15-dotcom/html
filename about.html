Established 1984

A Legacy of Scholarly Excellence

The Golden Library stands as the academic crown of the university. Founded to nurture intellectual curiosity, rigorous investigation, and lifelong learning, our facility bridges classical wisdom with digital research frontiers.

Housed in an expansive four-storey neoclassical building, the library houses over 52,000 print volumes, rare manuscripts, and institutional digital repository terminals.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions regarding student borrowing privileges, book renewals, and repository access.

How do I register for a library borrowing card?

Undergraduate and postgraduate students are issued an active library barcode during university orientation. You can also register your account online through our Membership Registration Portal using your college admission roll number.

Can I renew borrowed books online?

Yes. Students may log in to their student portal before the due date and submit a renewal request, provided no other student has placed a hold on that volume.

What are the overdue fine policies?

To promote fair circulation, an overdue fee of Rs. 2.00 per day per book is charged after the 14-day borrowing duration. A 2-day grace period is provided at the end of each semester.

How does the Book Bank scheme work?

The Book Bank provides full-semester textbook sets to qualifying meritorious and reservation-category scholars. Applications open on the ground floor circulation counter during the first two weeks of each semester.
